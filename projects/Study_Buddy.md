ICS 314 group project that allowed us to test or knowledge of frontend, backend, database connectivity, and project management fundamentals that culminated into a webiste published through Vercel.

Together with Wilson Hunyh, Ralph Ramos, and Reo Nagahama, we created UHM Study Buddy. This website allows students and faculty to easily create study sessions for others to join. By making a profile, you can create study sessions, add your friends as Study Buddies and share your favorite study playlists. Sessions are made by course adn you can join a study session if you are currently taking or have  taken that course previously. You can select a time and location so that others studying the same topic can join. Studying is better with a buddy!

A link to the deployed project on Vercel [here](https://study-buddy-inky-nine.vercel.app/)
